            What are FODMAPs?

            FODMAPs are fermentable carbs — Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides And Polyols — that can trigger bloating, gas, and pain for people with IBS or FODMAP sensitivity. This app helps you spot which foods are high or low FODMAP.

            The five FODMAP groups

            Fructans
            Wheat, onion, garlic
            GOS
            Legumes, cashews, pistachios
            Lactose
            Milk, soft cheese, yogurt
            Excess fructose
            Honey, apples, mango
            Polyols
            Stone fruit, mushrooms, sweeteners

            How to read a food

            • Enjoy Low FODMAP — safe in normal servings.
            • Careful Low FODMAP only in a small serving — watch the portion.
            • Avoid High FODMAP — best avoided during elimination.

            Portion is key: many foods are safe in a small serving but high FODMAP in a large one. Always check the serving guidance.

            Install on your iPhone

            In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. The app then opens full-screen like a native app and works offline.

            Important

            This app is for general education only and is not medical advice. FODMAP tolerance is individual, and thresholds are based on published Monash University research. Work with a doctor or dietitian, especially before the reintroduction phase.
