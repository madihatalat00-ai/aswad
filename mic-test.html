🎙️ Mic Doctor

Diagnoses whether your speakerphone voice problem is software or hardware. Run the four tests in order, answer each question, and you'll get a verdict at the end. Use your normal speaking voice, no headphones connected.

Setup Step 0

Tap the button and allow microphone access when your phone asks. If your phone lists more than one microphone, you can switch between them for any test.

Test A — Normal call mic processing ON

Records 6 seconds with the phone's voice processing enabled — closest to a normal call. Hold the phone like you're on speakerphone (flat, ~30 cm from your mouth) and count out loud from 1 to 10.

live level
Play it back. Can you hear your voice clearly?

Test B — Raw microphone processing OFF

Same recording but with all software audio processing disabled (echo cancellation, noise suppression, auto gain). If Test A was bad but this one is clear, software processing is eating your voice.

live level
Play it back. Can you hear your voice clearly?

Test C — Speakerphone simulation key test

This reproduces your exact problem. The app plays a tone out of your loudspeaker while recording you. Turn your media volume up first. Speak over the tone, counting 1 to 10. If your voice vanishes from this recording, we've reproduced the fault outside the phone call app.

live level
Play it back. Can you hear your voice (not just the tone)?

Test D — Top vs bottom mic hardware check

Most phones use the bottom mic for normal calls and the top/rear mic for speakerphone. Start the live meter, then gently scratch next to the bottom mic hole (bottom edge, near the charging port) and watch the meter jump. Then scratch next to the top mic hole (top edge / near the rear camera). A mic hole that produces no meter response is blocked or dead.

live level
Did the meter respond strongly at both mic locations?

Privacy: everything runs locally in your browser. Recordings never leave your phone and are discarded when you close the page.

Note: a browser can't use the phone's actual call audio path, so this tests the microphones and audio processing generally. If everything passes here but calls still fail, that itself is a strong clue — see the verdict.
